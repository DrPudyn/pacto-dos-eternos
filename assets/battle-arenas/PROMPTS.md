# Arenas de batalha — prompts usados

Ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. Cada fonte foi copiada para este projeto. PNGs de runtime preparados em grade 640x360 e ampliados com nearest-neighbor.

## Castelo 1

Production asset for a polished indie PIXEL ART RPG, battle background only. WIDE LANDSCAPE 16:9, full bleed, no border, no UI, no writing, NO characters, NO enemies. Scene: corrupted ancient forest clearing inside ruined stone walls, twisted trees in the upper corners, mossy cobblestones, small crimson roots at the outer edges, fireflies, jade-green foliage and warm amber lanterns. IMPORTANT camera: straight-on three-quarter side battle perspective like classic 16-bit JRPG battle backgrounds. Horizon/back wall at upper 25 percent; a LARGE UNOBSTRUCTED PLAYABLE FLAT FLOOR occupies middle and lower 70 percent. Four small party heroes will stand on left half at floor height 50-65 percent; enemies right half at same heights. Compose lighting and perspective so characters feel physically planted on this floor. Foreground lowest 20 percent is dark low-detail floor for a transparent skill hotbar overlay, NOT a black blank strip. No central props obstructing combat, no big central doors occupying the battlefield, no floating island, no steep isometric overhead perspective. Authentic coarse pixel art at logical 640x360 resolution enlarged nearest-neighbor, clearly square pixels, limited palette, stepped clusters and hand-dithered shading, clean readable shapes, black-edged details. Match tiny chibi fantasy RPG sprites. Elegant environmental storytelling at far edges, atmospheric depth, saturated accents, excellent art direction. Avoid photorealism, smooth 3D renders, painterly brushwork, fine noisy illustration, gaussian blur, gradients. Keep central battlefield floor visually quiet and make architecture inhabit the background.
