## Prompt final

Use case: ui-mockup / premium fantasy pixel-art game home screen.
Create a breathtaking but usable COMPLETE GAME HOME SCREEN MOCKUP for the original Portuguese fantasy RPG "PACTO DOS ETERNOS". This is a proposed redesign named "REFÚGIO DOS ETERNOS". Landscape 16:9 composition. A fully realized original isometric floating fortress village acts as the menu, NOT a row of ordinary menu cards.

VISUAL WORLD: One cohesive floating island fortress suspended above a dark sea of indigo clouds at blue hour, enormous faceted luminous azure crystal embedded beneath its broken rocky underside, thick aged golden chains hanging into the clouds. A huge atmospheric eclipsed moon behind distant mountains, tiny distant floating ruins. Deep blue stone architecture, navy slate roofs, aged brass and copper trim, original banners bearing an open book embracing a FOUR-pointed star. Warm amber windows and lanterns contrast with the cool blue surroundings. Spectacular atmosphere, carefully restrained magical particles. Authentic masterful handcrafted PIXEL ART: visible consistent square pixel clusters, crisp stepped outlines, block shading, limited coherent palette, rich material details, beautiful miniature architecture. No smooth 3D render, no blurry digital painting, no photographic textures, no vector-like illustration.

COMPOSITION / FUNCTION: The village fills the main screen with a clear central cobblestone plaza and branching paths. Seven spatially separated and immediately identifiable clickable landmarks, each with ONE compact handsome dark-blue and brass nameplate with crisp readable Portuguese pixel typography:
1. Left-center fortified barracks, training dummies, weapon racks, tiny sword/spear/bow heroes and a female mage outside. Label "HERÓIS".
2. Rear-left separate ruined arch containing a cracked ominous violet portal with faint monster eyes. Label "MASMORRA".
3. CENTERPIECE a beautiful raised circular arcane altar with a floating blue-and-gold grimoire, a few orbiting gold pact seals and subtle four-pointed-star runes. Label "PACTOS".
4. Front-right a distinctive ornate dragon-faced stone forge, small controlled channels of orange lava under a bridge, smoking chimney, tiny smith and navy-clad female assistant. Label "FORJAS".
5. Right-center a charming blue-canopy bazaar with displayed ornate weapons, gold lanterns, wooden stalls. Label "BAZAR", small secondary text "LOJA · MERCADO".
6. In the distant upper-right, on a connected smaller outcrop, a towering mysterious spire disappearing into clouds, accessible visually via a bridge. Label "TORRE DO INFINITO".
7. Front-left a triumphant brass trophy monument with three small podiums and original banners, a different silhouette from the barracks. Label "RANKING".

All landmarks fully inside the screen, no cropped labels. Keep landmarks separated by quiet paths, avoid visual clutter and overlapping text. Don't place the central altar or another building in front of labels. The crystal and sky enrich the scene without making the actual village too small.

INTERFACE integrated around the edges: slim elegant dark navy / aged gold top bar occupying at most 9% height. Left tiny original pixel hero avatar, exact title "PACTO DOS ETERNOS", smaller subtitle "REFÚGIO DOS ETERNOS". Right a gold coin icon with "12.500", a small ornate treasure-chest button, a small cog settings button. Bottom left two beautiful small PIXEL ART object shortcuts next to each other: blue/gold BOOK labeled "CÓDICE" and blue/gold BACKPACK labeled "MOCHILA". Keep bottom-right mostly clear and village center unobstructed. No giant call-to-action rectangle, no explanatory prose, no fake browser chrome, no watermark.

Aim for a lovingly crafted high-end pixel RPG hub: a place the player wants to inhabit, awe-inspiring on first view, architecturally coherent, intuitive navigation, polished and legible. Render only this finished proposed screen, no surrounding presentation board.
