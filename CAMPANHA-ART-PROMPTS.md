## campaign-frontier.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a premium original pixel-art game background, horizontal 16:9, for Pacto dos Eternos: FRONTEIRA DAS CINZAS. No text, no interface, no characters. Exquisite crisp pixel clusters, coherent outlines and block shadows, richly crafted fantasy pixel art matching a luxurious blue-and-gold floating medieval refuge. Camera side-front slight elevated depth suitable for a horizontal army battle. Foreground and lower middle 55% a broad empty readable weathered cobblestone road/plaza, no obstacles in fighting area. At upper right a monumental ruined fortress gate with dark roofs and amber lit windows, far distant corrupted castle silhouette. Left side destroyed stone arches and scorched trees, pale gold twilight sky, distant mountain layers. Blue slate stone, rich sunset amber, restrained violet corruption near fortress. Decor concentrated at edges and background. Small braziers and torn banners at far left and right, not central. Atmospheric but precise pixels, no blur, no photographic or painterly finish. Dramatic visual storytelling worthy of flagship fantasy game. This image will receive independent animated clouds, fire, banners and fog in engine; structures must be stable.
